Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

This application ("LooseAI Automation") is a personal automation tool that posts AI-generated content to the Instagram account @looseai.feed and manages replies to comments on that account.

What data is accessed

This application accesses the Instagram Business account's own posts and comments through the official Instagram Graph API, solely to publish content and respond to comments on that same account.

Data storage

This application does not store, sell, or share any personal data belonging to Instagram users who comment on or interact with the account. Comment text is processed temporarily and in real time to generate a reply, and is not retained afterward.

Third-party services used

This application uses the following third-party services to function: Groq (text generation), Hugging Face (image generation), ImgBB (temporary image hosting), and Meta's Instagram Graph API (publishing and comment management).

Contact

For questions about this application, contact the account owner via Instagram at @looseai.feed.
